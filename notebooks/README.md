# Computational Notebooks

This directory contains reproducible computational studies of statistical and numerical methods, developed throughout ME524 — Computational Statistics at UNICAMP.

The notebooks focus on the connection between **statistical theory, numerical implementation, and empirical validation**. Rather than reproducing lecture notes, each study develops a method from its mathematical motivation to a working implementation and investigates its behavior through simulation and diagnostics.

A typical notebook follows the workflow:

**Problem → Formulation → Algorithm → Implementation → Experiment → Diagnostics → Conclusions**

Across the notebooks, the emphasis is on:

* translating mathematical and statistical formulations into computational procedures;
* implementing and validating statistical algorithms;
* investigating convergence, numerical stability, and sources of error;
* designing reproducible experiments to compare theoretical and empirical behavior;
* interpreting computational results rather than treating implementation as a black box.

## Topics

The collection progressively covers Monte Carlo methods, random variable generation, numerical linear algebra, optimization, resampling methods, and Markov chain Monte Carlo.

Notebooks are refined as new methods and connections are introduced throughout the course.
